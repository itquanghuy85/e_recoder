Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

In short: your videos stay on your phone. E Screen Recorder has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and sends no data off your device.

The release build has no Internet permission. The app does not declare INTERNET, so technically it cannot upload your data to any server.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not upload videos, audio, screenshots or projects to any server.
  • It uses no accounts, advertising IDs, analytics or automatic crash reporting.
  • It shows no ads and does not sell or share data with third parties.
  • It does not read your messages, contacts, location, photos or other files.

Only when you tap Share is the file you chose sent to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. From then on the file belongs to the receiving app.

5. Sensitive content on screen

Your screen may show passwords, messages, phone numbers or financial details. Review your video before sharing and use the cover tool in the editor to hide sensitive information. The marker dot and markers drawn while recording in "small dot" mode are recorded into the video; "clean" mode records nothing but the real screen. Apps that protect their content (for example banking apps) appear black in recordings — that is Android's protection at work.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete videos in the app's Library, or with your Photos/Files app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes edit projects and settings. Exported videos live in Movies/Pictures and are yours to manage.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to delete on a server.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before the new version ships, and the date at the top will change.
