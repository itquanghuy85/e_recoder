Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything or show ads?

No. It is completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where are my videos saved?

In Movies/E Screen Recorder on your device, and they appear in the app's Library.

Why is my video black?

The app you recorded protects its content (for example banking or some streaming apps). Android blocks recording those screens.

The marker dot or control bar ended up in the video. How do I get a clean video?

Go to Settings → Markers → choose Record clean, edit later. Nothing shows on screen while recording; markers are drawn in the editor.

How do viewers see the marker before I tap?

Tap the small corner dot (or the hidden zone in clean mode), touch where you are about to press, then press for real. The cue ring closes in on the target half a second before the marker appears.

Recording stops when I switch apps (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Huawei)?

Turn off battery optimisation for E Screen Recorder in system Settings → Battery and allow auto-start. The app can open the right screen from Settings → Permissions.

I want all my data deleted.

Delete the videos in the Library and uninstall the app. We keep no data on any server.
