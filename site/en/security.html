Security

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

The safest way to protect your data is to never let it leave your device. That is how E Screen Recorder is built.

No way out to the network

The release build requests no Internet permission. No servers, no accounts, no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Minimal permissions, asked at the right time

Microphone only if you choose sound. Screen capture is confirmed by Android every time. No access to photos, contacts, messages or location.

Saved where Android wants it

Videos are saved through MediaStore into the app's own folder under Movies; no broad storage access is needed.

Cover tool for secrets

Covers added in the editor are burned into the exported video and cannot be removed from the exported file.

What to know while recording

  • System notifications, passwords or messages that appear on screen end up in the video. Turn on Do Not Disturb before recording.
  • Apps that protect their content (banking, some video players) appear black in recordings.
  • Review the video and cover sensitive information before sharing.

App integrity

  • Distributed through Google Play, signed with a private key using Play App Signing. Do not install the app from other sources.
  • Code is shrunk and obfuscated (R8) in release builds.
  • The app targets the latest Android release and follows Android's background-service restrictions.

Report a vulnerability

If you find a vulnerability, please send a description and steps to reproduce to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We will respond and fix it as soon as possible. Please do not disclose publicly before a fix is available.
